Privacy Policy

Who are we?

Tatami is published by Théo Bargue, 52-54 avenue Montjovis, 87000 Limoges, France. For any question about your data: sparyourmates@gmail.com.

Last updated: 9 October 2026.

The data we collect

Account: email address, password (never readable; it is protected by our host), name, username, country of residence.

Profile (optional): photo, bio, height, weight, club, sports practised. Your height and weight are visible to other users.

Activity: sessions (sport, duration, rounds, difficulty, club, description, photos), likes, follows, favourites, mutes, blocks, reports.

Private messages exchanged with other users.

Location: used only when you open the map, to show gyms and calculate a route. It is not stored on our servers, unless you choose to show yourself to your friends on the map: in that case, your rounded position (about 100 m) is stored and is visible only to people you follow who follow you back, until you turn the option off.

Certification (optional): the organisation and proof link you provide to get the “Certified fighter” badge.

Competitions, groups and challenges: your registrations, your attendance confirmed by the organiser, your result (medal, wins, losses, category), your group memberships, your challenge participations and your messages in groups. Your record is visible on your profile to people who can view it (everyone if your account is public, your accepted followers if it is private).

Payments (if you register for a paid competition online): payment is processed by Stripe; we neither see nor store your card number. We keep the amount, the date and the competition concerned.

Notifications: an identifier of your device, if you accept notifications.

Weigh-in, injury and technique journal (optional): weight, target category, injuries, points to fix and sparring results that you note in the app; they are visible only to you.

Stories: the photos and captions you post for 24 hours are visible to you and your accepted followers. Only you see the list of people who viewed them. They are deleted automatically once they expire.

Club pages (optional): if you create a professional page or claim a gym listing (“This is my club”), the information and proof you provide are reviewed by our team; the proof is seen only by them.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Tatami reads, with your permission, your sleep, heart rate (at rest and during workouts), heart rate variability and workouts. Tatami writes nothing to Health.

This data stays on your iPhone: it is used to compute the “Ready to train?” indicator and to import your workouts, and is not sent to any server or shared with other users. An imported workout becomes an ordinary Tatami session (duration, calories, average heart rate in the note) and follows the visibility rules of your sessions.

You can withdraw access at any time in the Health app (Profile picture > Privacy > Apps > Tatami).

Why?

To run the app: create your account, record your sessions, display the feed, profiles, map and messaging.

To ensure safety and moderation: handle reports and block abuse.

To estimate the calories of a session from its duration, intensity and your weight. This is an indicative estimate, not medical advice.

To compute your Elo, your league and the rankings, and to check that results are not rigged (anti-cheat rules applied automatically).

To limit abuse: a maximum number of actions per minute and per hour is applied to prevent spam.

To send you the notifications you have accepted (messages, competitions, challenges, groups, weekly recap).

Who receives your data?

Our technical providers: hosting and database (Supabase), the map, place search and routes (Apple Maps), online payments (Stripe), sending notifications (Apple and Expo) and distributing the app (Apple, Expo).

Other users see your name, username, photo, bio, height, weight, sessions and published photos. Your private messages are visible only to you and your recipient.

We do not sell your data.

Data sources

The combat sports gyms shown on the map come from Apple Maps, pages created by clubs in Tatami and the open Overture Maps Foundation database (overturemaps.org), whose data comes in particular from Meta, Microsoft and BrightQuery (CDLA-Permissive-2.0 licence) and Foursquare (Apache-2.0 licence). This information (addresses, phone numbers, websites) may be inaccurate: check it with the club.

How long?

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, sessions, photos, videos, messages, registrations, groups and challenges are permanently erased.

The payment provider (Stripe) keeps payment data according to its own legal and accounting obligations, even after your account is deleted.

Technical traces used to limit abuse are automatically erased after a few days.

Your rights

You can view and edit your information in the app (You > Edit profile).

You can delete your account at any time (You > Account and sign out > Delete my account).

To exercise your other rights (access, rectification, portability, objection, restriction) or to lodge a complaint, write to us at sparyourmates@gmail.com. You can also contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Minors

Tatami is intended for people aged 16 and over. If you are under 16, ask your parents for permission before creating an account.

Security

Exchanges with our servers are encrypted. Access to data is protected by rules that allow each user to see only what is intended for them.

Changes

We may update this policy. In case of an important change, we will let you know in the app.
