Club pages

You may claim a gym listing only if you actually run that club. Our team reviews each request and the proof provided. A false claim leads to the request being refused, the page being removed and may lead to the account being suspended. In case of a dispute, we may remove or transfer a page.

Competitions, groups and challenges

Competitions are run by independent organisers: SPAR provides the tool but does not organise events and is not responsible for how they run. The organiser confirms attendance and enters results; they undertake to enter only accurate information.

Elo earned through a competition follows automatic rules (publication at least 3 days in advance, minimum number of confirmed participants, monthly caps). A rigged result leads to the points being removed and may lead to the suspension of the accounts involved.

Administrators of a group or challenge can moderate its members and messages. You can leave a group at any time.

Payments

When a competition is paid online, payment is made through Stripe and goes to the organiser, with SPAR taking a commission. You can be refunded and withdraw up to 7 days before the start; if the organiser cancels, all registrants are refunded. A duplicate payment is refunded automatically.

Competitions paid on site are paid directly to the organiser, outside the app.

“Certified fighter” badge

It is granted after your signature is verified with an organisation. A false declaration leads to the request being refused, the badge being removed and may lead to the account being suspended.

Calories, Elo and rankings are indicative estimates, not medical advice or official results. Gym hours and information come from third-party sources and may be inaccurate: check them with the gym.
